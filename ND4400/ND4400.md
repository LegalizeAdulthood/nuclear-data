# ND4400 Series Multichannel Analyzers

The ND4400 series multichannel analyzers incorporated an
[ND812](../ND812/ND812.md) minicomputer as part of a complete system. The
ND4400 was a single-detector gamma-ray spectroscopy system. Software could be
written in ND812 assembly language or NUTRAN; a FORTRAN implementation became
available later but was reportedly not very successful.

A former Nuclear Data Product Support Manager recalled using and programming
an ND4400 while working at the University of Michigan before joining Nuclear
Data.
